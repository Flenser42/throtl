# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Throtl project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with other people.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
take any action they consider appropriate, up to and including a temporary or
permanent ban from the project spaces.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer through GitHub —
mention `@Flenser42` or open a private report via the repository's **Security**
tab if the matter is sensitive. Reports will be reviewed and handled as
confidentially as possible.
